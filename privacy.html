Privacy

Last updated October 17, 2026

Champion 5 is a small daily game. We only keep what we need to run it, we don't sell anything, and you can delete your account whenever you want.

What we collect

  • Your email, so you can log in and reset your password.
  • Your username, which other players see.
  • Your password, stored scrambled (hashed). Nobody can read it, including us.
  • Your games: the answers you typed and when, your scores, wins and losses, rating and streak.

What other players can see

  • Your username, rating, rank and record on the leaderboard.
  • Your daily score on today's leaderboard.
  • Your answers and score for a match, when someone plays against your run as their opponent.

Your email is never shown to anyone.

What we never do

  • Sell or share your information
  • Show ads
  • Track you across other websites
  • Send marketing emails

Who helps run the site

  • Supabase stores accounts and game data.
  • Vercel hosts the website and counts visits without cookies or personal details.
  • Resend sends password-reset emails.

They only handle your information to provide those services to us.

Your choices

  • Delete your account any time from the home screen: tap your username, then Delete my account. This permanently removes your email, username and every game you've played.
  • Get a copy of your data or ask a question by emailing hello@champion5.site.

Age

Champion 5 isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information. If you think a child has made an account, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If this page changes, we'll update the date at the top. Big changes will also be announced on the site.

Questions? hello@champion5.site
